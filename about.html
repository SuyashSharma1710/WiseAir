Our Mission: Engineering a Healthier India

We are a team of engineers, designers, and environmental experts committed to solving one of India's greatest challenges: air pollution. Our goal is to create safe, breathable environments where communities can thrive.

A Legacy of German Engineering, Proudly Made in India.

AirFinery is born from a strategic partnership between **MRH Technologies (a subsidiary of OK Play)** and **MANN+HUMMEL**, Germany's world-renowned filtration leader since 1941. This collaboration combines an 84-year legacy of engineering excellence with robust Indian manufacturing to create a solution built for our environment, proudly supporting the 'Make in India' initiative.

+

Scientifically Validated by IIT Delhi

Recommended by the Prime Minister’s Office, IIT Delhi was appointed to evaluate our technology. Their independent, 60-day assessment during peak pollution periods confirmed the real-world efficacy of our solutions.

80%+ PM Reduction

Results showed a significant reduction of over 80% in both PM2.5 and PM10 levels across indoor and outdoor test areas.

AQI Improvement

Air Quality Index was drastically improved, moving from "Very Poor" (301-400) down to "Good" (0-50).

Proven in Extreme Conditions

The tests were conducted during Delhi's toughest pollution periods, including stubble burning and post-Diwali smog, proving our technology's resilience and effectiveness even when AQI was "Severe" (>401).

Our Vision: Clean Air for Every Child

We believe every child has the right to breathe clean air. Our primary mission is to transform our schools and communities into safe havens, free from the harmful effects of air pollution, ensuring a healthier future for the next generation. We are making 2025 the year of climate action.
