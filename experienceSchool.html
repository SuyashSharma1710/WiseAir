Why Clean Air is Essential for Your School.

Children are uniquely vulnerable to air pollution. They breathe more rapidly than adults, inhaling more pollutants which can impact cognitive development, reduce concentration, and lead to long-term health issues like asthma and allergies.

  • ✓ Beyond the Classroom: Pollutants affect students not just indoors, but in hallways, cafeterias, and playgrounds.
  • ✓ Impact on Learning: Poor air quality is linked to reduced attention spans and increased absenteeism.
  • ✓ Total Campus Health: Clean air is equally crucial for the well-being of your teachers, staff, and visiting parents.
